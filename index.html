The Magpie Ring Files. A globe-trotting detective game.

How it works
  1. Something precious has been stolen. Question witnesses in the city where it happened.
  2. Their answers hint at where the thief went next: a currency, a flag, a language, a landmark. Fly there. A wrong turn costs hours.
  3. Longer interviews also reveal details about the thief. Enter them in the dossier until only one member of the Ring fits, then request a warrant.
  4. Catch up with the thief, warrant in hand, before the clock runs out.

An original game. All characters, organisations and stolen goods are fictional. The geography is real.
